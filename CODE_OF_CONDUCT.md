# Code of Conduct

fastEmbedR contributors and users are expected to communicate respectfully,
welcome technically grounded disagreement, and avoid harassment,
discrimination, personal attacks, and disclosure of private information.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation
when behavior undermines a safe and productive collaboration. Reports should be
sent privately to `tkcaccia@gmail.com`; include links or other context needed
to investigate. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with
attention to the safety and privacy of everyone involved.

This policy applies to project repositories, issue trackers, review channels,
and project-related public or private communication.
